# DAKSHI  
### Digital Knowledge Ecosystem for Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS)

**DAKSHI** is a scholarly digital knowledge initiative designed to preserve, structure, and disseminate Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) with academic rigor, civilizational sensitivity, and long-term sustainability.

Developed as part of an academic internship at **Delhi University (August 2023)**, the project focuses on **institutional knowledge design** rather than trend-driven content creation.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Modern digital spaces often oversimplify or ideologically distort Indian civilizational knowledge.  
DAKSHI addresses this gap by creating a **credible, structured, and academically governed digital ecosystem** that balances accessibility with source integrity.

---

## 🧠 Core Principles

- Academically serious and non-speculative  
- Free from anachronistic and ideological distortion  
- Preservation of original Sanskrit terminology  
- Multilingual and culturally inclusive  
- Designed as a long-term civilizational archive  

---

## 🏗️ What Was Designed

- Complete **information architecture** for a digital knowledge platform  
- **Gyandarshan** – a short-form daily knowledge system across history, philosophy, sciences, and scriptures  
- Framework for an **annual / biannual scholarly magazine**  
- Multi-layered **editorial governance and quality control system**  
- Sustainable **volunteer-driven research-to-publication workflow**

---

## 📚 Knowledge Domains

- History  
- Philosophy  
- Sciences  
- Scriptures  
- Governance & Economics  

---

## 🛡️ Editorial Governance

- Mandatory citations  
- No speculative claims or anachronism  
- Calm, scholarly tone  
- Multi-level editorial and fact-checking review  

---

## 🔮 Future Scope

- Peer-reviewed long-form research  
- Multilingual glossaries  
- Digitization of rare manuscripts  
- Academic partnerships  

---

## 📌 Project Nature

This project emphasizes **content architecture, editorial governance, and knowledge system design**, not ideological advocacy or opinion-based narratives.
